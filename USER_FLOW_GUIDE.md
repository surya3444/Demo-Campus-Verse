# CampusVerse — User Flow Guide

This document describes the complete user experience for **Students** and **Teachers** on the CampusVerse platform.

---

## Part 1: Student User Flow

### 1.1 Logging In

- The student opens the platform and enters their registered email and password.
- After successful login, they are taken directly to their **Dashboard**.
- If the student is part of only one campus/branch, that branch is automatically selected. If they belong to multiple branches, they choose one.

---

### 1.2 Student Dashboard

The dashboard is the student's home screen. It provides a quick overview of everything important:

- **Class & Section** — Shows which class and section the student belongs to.
- **Today's Schedule** — A timeline of today's classes with subject names, teacher names, and timings. The currently running period is highlighted.
- **Pending Work** — Count of assignments and quizzes that are yet to be completed, with upcoming due dates.
- **Attendance Summary** — Current month's attendance percentage with a breakdown of days present, absent, and late.
- **Fee Balance** — Outstanding fee amount, or a "Cleared" status if all fees are paid.
- **Hostel Info** — If the student has a hostel allocation, it shows the hostel name, room number, and bed number.
- **Recent Announcements** — Latest announcements from the institution.
- **Quick Actions** — Shortcut buttons to jump to Timetable, Assignments, Attendance, and Fee Details.

---

### 1.3 Academics

#### My Timetable

- Displays the weekly class schedule in a grid view.
- Each slot shows the subject, teacher name, and timing.
- The current day is highlighted for easy reference.
- Break, lunch, and assembly periods are also shown.

#### Assignments

- Lists all assignments and quizzes assigned to the student's section.
- Each entry shows the subject, due date, total marks, and current status.
- Status can be: **Answer** (not yet attempted), **Pending Review** (submitted, awaiting grading), or a **Score** (graded).
- Overdue and upcoming assignments are visually marked.
- Students can open an assignment to view questions and submit their answers.

#### Attendance

- Shows a history of daily attendance records.
- Students can filter by date range to view specific periods.
- Each day shows whether the student was **Present**, **Absent**, or **Late**, with colour-coded indicators.
- A summary section shows overall attendance percentage and counts.

#### Gradebook

- Displays all graded assignments and assessments.
- Summary at the top shows: total graded items, average percentage, best score, and lowest score.
- Scores are colour-coded: green for high (75%+), blue for moderate (50–74%), amber for low (30–49%), and red for very low (below 30%).
- Each entry shows the assignment title, subject, marks obtained out of total, percentage, and a pass/needs improvement indicator.
- Students can click on any entry to see detailed grade information.

---

### 1.4 Finance

#### Fee Details

- Shows a summary with three cards: **Total Fee**, **Amount Paid** (with a progress bar), and **Balance Due**.
- Displays the student's admission number, class, and section.
- Below the summary, each fee structure is listed with its installments.
- Each installment shows: name, due date, amount, amount paid, and status (Paid / Due / Upcoming).
- Colour-coded status icons help quickly identify what has been paid and what is pending.

---

### 1.5 Hostel

#### My Allocation

- If the student is allocated to a hostel, this page shows:
  - Hostel name
  - Room number and bed number
  - Allocation date and expected vacating date
  - Current status
- A history of past allocations (if any) is also displayed.
- If no hostel is allocated, a message indicates that there is no active allocation.

---

### 1.6 Communications

#### Announcements

- Lists all published announcements from the institution.
- Each announcement shows the title, message content, date posted, and priority level.
- Announcements are displayed in reverse chronological order (newest first).

#### Calendar

- A monthly calendar view showing school/college events.
- Students can navigate between months using previous/next buttons.
- A sidebar shows the next 5 upcoming events.
- Clicking on an event shows its title, description, date, time, location, and event type.

---

### 1.7 Campus Feed

- A social feed where students can see posts from the campus community.
- Students can **create new posts** with text and images.
- Students can **like** and **comment** on posts from others.
- Students can **reply** to comments in threads.
- Posts may have different audience settings depending on who published them.

---

### 1.8 Profile

- Students can view and edit their personal information:
  - First name and last name
  - Phone number
- Students can upload or change their profile photo.
- The profile page also displays the student's email, role, and institution name (read-only).

---

### 1.9 What Students Cannot Do

Students have view-only access to most features. They **cannot**:

- Create or edit assignments, quizzes, or lesson plans
- Mark attendance for anyone
- Make fee payments through the platform (they can only view fee status)
- Manage hostel allocations
- Create announcements or calendar events
- Access any administrative, HR, or staff management features
- View other students' personal information
- Moderate or delete content on the campus feed

## Part 2: Teacher User Flow

### 2.1 Logging In

- The teacher opens the platform and enters their registered email and password.
- After successful login, they are taken to their **Teacher Dashboard**.
- If the teacher is associated with multiple branches, they select the relevant one. Otherwise, it is auto-selected.
- If it is the teacher's first login and a password change is required, they will be prompted to set a new password before proceeding.

---

### 2.2 Teacher Dashboard

The teacher dashboard provides a daily operational overview:

- **Today's Classes** — Number of classes scheduled for the day.
- **Unmarked Attendance** — Number of classes where attendance has not yet been marked (action needed).
- **Pending Grading** — Number of assignments waiting to be graded, with student counts.
- **Today's Timetable** — A timeline of the day's teaching periods, breaks, lunch, and assembly slots.
- **Upcoming Parent-Teacher Meetings** — Any scheduled PTM events.
- **Leave Balance** — Breakdown of available leave by type: Casual, Sick, Earned, Maternity, and Loss of Pay.
- **Personal Stats** — Total periods per week, total sections assigned, and today's completed vs. remaining periods.
- **Quick Actions** — Shortcut buttons to view timetable, my classes, lesson plans, assignments, and apply for leave.

---

### 2.3 Teaching

#### My Classes

- Shows all classes and sections assigned to the teacher.
- Each class card displays the section name, number of students, and subjects taught.
- Clicking on a class opens the detailed view with the student list and class-specific information.

#### My Timetable

- Displays the teacher's weekly teaching schedule.
- Shows which periods are assigned, the subject, class/section, and timing.
- Helps teachers plan their week at a glance.

#### Lesson Plans

- Teachers can **create**, **edit**, and **delete** lesson plans.
- A lesson plan includes structured content for a particular subject and class.
- Teachers can view all their existing lesson plans in a list or grid view.
- New lesson plans can be created from scratch using the "New Lesson Plan" option.

#### Assignments

- Teachers can **create**, **edit**, and **delete** assignments and quizzes for their classes.
- When creating an assignment, teachers can:
  - Set a title, description, subject, and class/section
  - Add questions
  - Set due dates and total marks
  - Publish when ready
- Teachers can **view student submissions** for each assignment.
- Teachers can **grade** submitted work and provide marks.

#### Gradebook

- Teachers can **view** and **enter grades** for students in their assigned classes.
- Allows editing of previously entered grades.
- Shows a consolidated view of student performance across assignments.

#### My Courses (Online Courses)

- Teachers can **create** and **manage** online courses for digital/remote teaching.
- Within a course, teachers can:
  - Add and organize **lessons** with content
  - Create **quizzes** for assessment
  - View **enrolled students** and track their progress
- Course reports provide insights into student engagement and completion.

---

### 2.4 Attendance

#### Mark Attendance

- Teachers can mark daily attendance for students in their assigned classes.
- For each student, teachers can set the status as: **Present**, **Absent**, **Late**, or **Half-Day**.
- Teachers can add remarks for individual students if needed.
- Once attendance is finalized/locked, it cannot be modified further.

#### View Attendance

- Teachers can view attendance records for their classes.
- Records can be filtered by date to review past attendance.

#### Holidays

- Teachers can view the list of declared holidays for the academic year.

---

### 2.5 HR & Self-Service

#### Leave Management

- Teachers can **apply for leave** by selecting the leave type, dates, and providing a reason.
- Available leave types include: Casual Leave, Sick Leave, Earned Leave, Maternity Leave, and Loss of Pay.
- Teachers can **view their leave history** with statuses: Pending, Approved, or Rejected.
- A leave balance summary shows how many days are available, used, and pending for each leave type.

#### My Payslips

- Teachers can **view their own salary payslips** (they cannot view anyone else's).
- Payslips are organized by month and can be filtered by year.
- Each payslip shows:
  - Earnings breakdown (salary components)
  - Deductions breakdown (tax, insurance, etc.)
  - Working days, days present, and loss-of-pay days
  - Gross earnings, total deductions, and net payable amount
  - Payment status and bank details

#### Workload

- Teachers can view their assigned workload, including:
  - Subjects and classes assigned
  - Number of periods per week for each class/section
  - Department and designation information

---

### 2.6 Communications

#### Announcements

- Teachers can **view** announcements published by the institution.
- Teachers **cannot** create or manage announcements (this is reserved for administrators).

#### Calendar

- Teachers can view the school/college event calendar.
- Shows upcoming events, important dates, and scheduled activities.

---

### 2.7 Campus Feed

- Teachers can **view posts** from the campus community.
- Teachers can **create new posts** with text and images.
- Teachers can **like** and **comment** on posts.
- Teachers can **reply** to comments in threads.
- Teachers **cannot** moderate, pin, or delete posts (this is reserved for administrators).

---

### 2.8 Profile

- Teachers can view and edit their personal information:
  - First name and last name
  - Phone number
- Teachers can upload or change their profile photo.
- The profile page also displays the teacher's email, role, and institution name (read-only).

---

### 2.9 What Teachers Cannot Do

Teachers have access to their own teaching tools and self-service HR features. They **cannot**:

- Access organization settings, branch management, or user/role management
- View or manage admissions (leads, applications, pipeline)
- Access finance operations (fee collection, fee structure creation, approvals)
- Manage hostel allocations, rooms, or hostels
- Create announcements or calendar events
- Approve or reject leave requests from other staff (reserved for HR managers)
- View payslips of other staff members
- Access health centre management
- Issue or manage certificates
- Perform bulk student uploads, promotions, or transfers
- Moderate content on the campus feed
